Privacy Policy

Vega Pocket Assistant

Effective 15 November 2026 · Updated for 1.1 (dictation, reminders, widgets, two companion apps) · Applies to the iOS app “Vega Pocket Assistant”

Support Contact

The short version. Pocket Assistant keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your messages are handed to a companion app you install on the same device — Pocket LLM Chat or Pocket LLMG — for generation, and the replies are stored in a local database that only you can open.

1. What the app stores on your device

  • Records you create or that are extracted from a reply: tasks, notes, expenses, events, people and ideas, including titles, text, amounts, dates, tags and the record's status.
  • Conversation history: your message and the assistant's reply, so the chat can be shown again after a restart.
  • Reminders: the due times of the tasks and events the app has scheduled as local notifications.
  • Small settings: the selected assistant provider, the dictation engine, pending request identifiers, and whether the one-time unlock was purchased.
  • Share container files: short-lived JSON files used to hand a request to the companion app and to receive its reply. They are deleted as soon as they are consumed, and any leftover is removed after 24 hours.

All of this lives inside the app's own container (and the shared App Group container described below). It is protected by iOS Data Protection and cannot be read while the device is locked.

A JSON backup is written only when you choose Export backup, and only to the location you pick in the Files app. Reading it back in is equally voluntary.

3. Dictation (optional)

Voice input is optional and off until you press the microphone. Two engines can provide it, chosen in Settings → Voice input:

  • Whisper (default). Audio is transcribed on this iPhone by a model downloaded once; the recording is discarded afterwards. Nothing is written to disk and nothing is sent to the companion app — only the text you approve in the message field is sent. The model download (Hugging Face or its mirror when that host is unreachable) contains no personal data, no audio and no text, and the model can be deleted at any time.
  • Apple Speech. If you choose the system recogniser instead, the audio is processed by iOS speech recognition. Apple's privacy terms apply to that processing, and it may use network data. We receive nothing either way; the app only gets the transcript it puts in the message field.

Microphone permission is requested when you first press the microphone, and speech recognition permission when Apple Speech is used. Both can be revoked in iOS Settings; dictation then stops working and nothing else changes.

No audio is ever stored, shared or used for training by this app.

4. What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, sign-in, email capture or contact upload.
  • No access to your photos, contacts, calendar or health data.
  • The microphone and speech recognition are used only for the optional dictation described in section 3 — nowhere else — and both permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and therefore no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with third parties.

5. Reminders, notifications and widgets

Reminders are local notifications: the app asks iOS to show them, and nothing is sent anywhere. A task reminds you at its due time and an event at its start; up to 60 are scheduled at a time, soonest first. The list of scheduled reminders is stored on the device with the records themselves, and turning notifications off in iOS Settings stops them without affecting anything else.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (Quick Ask, Records, Search) read the same local database through the app's own code. They show only titles and dates, keep nothing of their own, and cannot open the database to any other app or to the widget extension's own storage.

6. Diagnostics and logging

Release builds log only technical identifiers such as a request ID, a status word and timings. They never log your message text, reply text, record contents or amounts to the system log. Debug builds, which are only used during development, can show request and reply details on screen and in the log.

7. Purchases

Pocket Assistant offers one optional non-consumable purchase that removes the record limit. Payment is processed entirely by Apple. The app only stores a local flag indicating whether the purchase is active, derived from StoreKit's current entitlements. We never see your payment method or billing details.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay until you delete them. Archive moves a record out of the list without deleting it; Delete moves it to the Deleted view, where you can restore it or delete it for good.
  • Delete All Records in the Records menu and Erase all data in Settings both remove records from the device immediately and cannot be undone.
  • Erase all data in Settings removes every record, every message and every pending request from the device immediately.
  • Deleting the app removes its container, including the local database.
  • Shared request and reply files are deleted after consumption, with a 24-hour backstop for leftovers.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it does not collect information at all.

10. Your rights

Because the app transfers no personal data to us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on our side. You control all app data directly on your device through the records list and Settings → Erase all data. If you have a question about this policy, write to the address below.

11. Changes

If a future version starts sending data off the device — for example an optional cloud provider — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be visible in the app and on this page.
