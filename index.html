Privacy Policy

Vega Pocket Assistant

Effective 15 November 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Vega Pocket Assistant” (bundle ID com.vega.personalassistant.app)

Support Contact

The short version. Pocket Assistant keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your messages are handed to the Pocket LLM app on the same device for generation, and the replies are stored in a local database that only you can open.

1. What the app stores on your device

  • Records you create or that are extracted from a reply: tasks, notes, expenses, events, people and ideas, including titles, text, amounts, dates, tags and the record's status.
  • Conversation history: your message and the assistant's reply, so the chat can be shown again after a restart.
  • Small settings: the selected assistant provider, pending request identifiers, and whether the one-time unlock was purchased.
  • Share container files: short-lived JSON files used to hand a request to Pocket LLM and to receive its reply. They are deleted as soon as they are consumed, and any leftover is removed after 24 hours.

All of this lives inside the app's own container (and the shared App Group container described below). It is protected by iOS Data Protection and cannot be read while the device is locked.

2. What leaves your device

PathDataDestination

Nothing is uploaded to us. We operate no server, no database and no logging service for app content. If you use a build of Pocket LLM that downloads models from the internet, that download is handled by Pocket LLM and is outside this policy.

3. What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, sign-in, email capture or contact upload.
  • No access to your photos, contacts, calendar, location, microphone or health data. The app does not request those permissions.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and therefore no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with third parties.

4. Diagnostics and logging

Release builds log only technical identifiers such as a request ID, a status word and timings. They never log your message text, reply text, record contents or amounts to the system log. Debug builds, which are only used during development, can show request and reply details on screen and in the log.

5. Purchases

Pocket Assistant offers one optional non-consumable purchase that removes the record limit. Payment is processed entirely by Apple. The app only stores a local flag indicating whether the purchase is active, derived from StoreKit's current entitlements. We never see your payment method or billing details.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay until you delete them. Deleted records are marked deleted and are removed permanently when the database is compacted or when you erase all data.
  • Erase all data in Settings removes every record, every message and every pending request from the device immediately.
  • Deleting the app removes its container, including the local database.
  • Shared request and reply files are deleted after consumption, with a 24-hour backstop for leftovers.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it does not collect information at all.

8. Your rights

Because the app transfers no personal data to us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on our side. You control all app data directly on your device through the records list and Settings → Erase all data. If you have a question about this policy, write to the address below.

9. Changes

If a future version starts sending data off the device — for example an optional cloud provider — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be visible in the app and on this page.
