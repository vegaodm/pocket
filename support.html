Getting started

Do I need another app?

Yes. Pocket Assistant does not include a language model. Install one of our two companion apps on the same iPhone and load a model there:

  • Pocket LLM Chat — choose between LFM3 1.7B (about 1.0 GB, recommended) and Bonsai 2B (about 1.2 GB).
  • Pocket LLMG — Gemma 4 E2B Instruct (text-only, 4-bit, about 2.5 GiB); it ships this one model.

Then in Pocket Assistant open Settings → Assistant provider and pick the app you installed. Both apps must be signed by the same developer so they can share the hand-off folder. Only one is needed; you can switch between them at any time.

Which model should I use?

It depends on the app you installed, and Settings → Recommended model always names the right one for the provider you picked.

  • Pocket LLM Chat — LFM3 1.7B is recommended: it follows the structured answer format reliably, including dates in your local timezone. Bonsai 2B is supported and uses much less memory, but it skips the structured part more often; Pocket Assistant then falls back to reading your message directly, so records are still saved — you just get no written summary.
  • Pocket LLMG — Gemma 4 E2B Instruct is the only model that app offers, so there is nothing to choose.
How should I phrase a message?

Records keep your own wording and language; nothing is translated. The app interface itself is English.

One thing per message works best:

  • “Remind me to send the invoice tomorrow at 9 AM”
  • “I spent 68 CNY on lunch with the team”
  • “Meeting with Tom on Friday at 9 AM about the mobile release”
  • “Idea: a weekly review every Friday afternoon”
  • “Note that the office Wi-Fi password is on the fridge”

To choose the kind, lead with the word:

  • Note — “Note that…”, “记录：…”, “记一下：…” keeps information you want to find again (a fact, a summary, a phone number).
  • Idea — “Idea:…”, “想法：…”, “灵感：…” records something to explore later (a possibility, a suggestion, a “what if”).
  • Task — “Remind me…”, “提醒我…”, “记得…” adds a to-do, with a due time when you give one.

Ask a question and say you want it kept, and the answer is filed as a note or as ideas:

  • “Explain daylight saving time in two sentences and save it as a note”
  • “解释一下为什么手机会发热，然后记一下”
  • “Give me three ideas for a weekend trip nearby and save them as ideas”
  • “帮我想几个减少开会的方法，存成想法”

The note keeps a short summary, not the whole answer, so it stays readable next to your other records.

Building a knowledge base

Ask for the same shape every time and the notes stay findable:

  • name the subject: “Save a note titled Compound interest: what it is and how it compounds, two sentences, tagged finance”
  • or in Chinese: “把复利是什么、怎么算的存成 note，标题就叫「复利」，加标签 finance”
  • ask for a summary, not a copy: “two sentences, standalone” keeps the record readable months later
  • brainstorm into separate records: “three ideas for cutting meetings, save as ideas, tag productivity”

Find them again with the Search tab: full-text search covers the title, body and tags, and the Records tab filters by Notes or Ideas.

To collect material on a topic, name the topic, say you want a note, and add a tag:

  • “以「分布式一致性」为主题整理成一条 note，两个要点，标签 distributed”
  • “Notes on compound interest — three bullets, tag finance”
  • “分别给 CI/CD 和蓝绿部署各写一条 note，标签 devops”

The companion app runs on your iPhone and has no internet access, so a note holds what the model already knows — it cannot look up today's news. Ask it to flag uncertainty when the topic is niche: “如果不确定就写明”.

A paragraph that mixes a meeting, a person and an expense is harder for a small model and produces rougher records.

Records

How do I put a record aside without deleting it?

Swipe a row rightwards (from its left edge) and tap Archive. Archived records leave the list and stop reminding you, but they are kept: the toolbar menu at the top left switches the whole screen to Archived, where swiping the other way brings one back with Unarchive.

I deleted something by accident

Delete only moves a record to the Deleted view in the same toolbar menu. Swipe it and tap Restore to bring it back. Nothing is removed for good until you delete it from there (Delete forever), empty the Deleted view, or erase all data.

How do I delete everything at once?

The toolbar menu in Records has Empty Deleted… (only the deleted records) and Delete All Records… (active, archived and deleted). Both show how many records they will remove and ask for confirmation. Settings → Erase all data goes further and also removes the conversation and any pending request.

Can I keep a conversation from the companion app as a record?

Yes, from the other side: in Pocket LLM Chat or Pocket LLMG choose Send chat to Pocket Assistant in the “…” menu. The conversation arrives in Records as a note you can edit, search and delete like any other record.

What does “Updated 1 record” mean?

A reply that changed a record you already had — for example “change the meeting to 6PM” — merges into the existing row instead of creating a second one. “Saved” means new records were added; “Updated” means existing ones were changed.

Reminders and widgets

How do I get a reminder?

Allow notifications when the app asks (or turn them on in iOS Settings → Pocket Assistant), then save a task with a due time or an event with a start time. The notification arrives at that time and tapping it opens the record. Up to 60 reminders are scheduled at once — soonest first; the rest are scheduled as earlier ones pass.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press the Home Screen, tap +, and search for Pocket Assistant. Three widgets are available in the small and medium sizes: Quick Ask (opens the composer), Records (recent records) and Search (jumps straight to search). They read the same local database and show titles and dates only.

Dictation

How do I dictate instead of typing?

Press and hold the microphone to the left of the message field, speak, then release. The text appears in the field — edit it if you like, then tap send. Nothing is sent until you do: dictation only fills the composer.

Which transcription should I choose?

Settings → Voice input offers two options:

  • Whisper tiny.en — a model downloaded once (about 40 MB) from Hugging Face, or its mirror if that host is unreachable. Transcription then runs entirely on the iPhone, including in airplane mode. Audio is never uploaded and never stored. This is the private default.
  • Apple Speech — the recogniser built into iOS. Nothing to download, but Apple's speech services are involved: their privacy terms apply and recognition may use network data. Pick this if you would rather not download a model.

You can switch at any time; the model you downloaded stays until you delete it.

Why did it ask to download something?

Whisper is an on-device model, so the first dictation needs it locally. The prompt offers three choices: download Whisper (about 40 MB), use Apple Speech instead, or cancel. If the main model host is unreachable the app switches to a mirror automatically, and the download can be resumed from Settings → Voice input.

Does dictation need the internet?

Whisper: only for that first download. Apple Speech: no download, but Apple's recogniser may use network data under their terms.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

With Whisper: no. Audio is transcribed on the device and discarded — never stored, never sent to the companion app, never used for training. With Apple Speech, the audio is handled by iOS speech recognition, which is governed by Apple's terms rather than ours.

The microphone button is greyed out

Apple Speech may be unavailable for your region or locale, and Whisper needs its model. Open Settings → Voice input: download the model, or switch the option. The keyboard always works.

Dictation stopped after a second or two

Recording ends automatically after about 1.2 seconds of silence, and stops at 60 seconds. Speak in one go, or press again for another sentence — the new text is appended to what you already have.

It misheard a word

Fix it in the field before sending — that is exactly why the transcript is editable. Short, single-topic sentences transcribe best, which also makes the records cleaner.

Which languages can I dictate?

English in this version. Typed input can be any language; the records keep whatever language you wrote in.

Common questions

Why does the app jump to another app and come back?

iOS allows only one app to be in the foreground, and the model lives in the companion app (Pocket LLM Chat or Pocket LLMG). Pocket Assistant writes your message to a shared folder, opens that app, waits for it to finish, and it hands the reply back. Pocket Assistant then updates the bubble. The companion app shows a short “Generating a reply…” screen during that hand-off instead of its own chat history.

The reply says it was saved but the model wrote nothing

That is the fallback: the model did not return a usable structured answer, so Pocket Assistant read your own message and saved the record itself. Nothing is lost; the record is marked for review in Records.

What is the free limit?

50 records. Reading, searching, editing and exporting are always free; the cap only applies to new records. One one-time purchase removes it, with no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another iPhone

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account, or use the Restore button on the unlock screen.

Where is my data? Can I back it up?

Everything is in a local database on your iPhone; there is no account and no cloud copy. Use Settings → Export backup to write a JSON file wherever you like, and Import backup to restore it on another device.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Erase all data removes every record, message and pending request from the device immediately. To clear records only, use the Records toolbar menu (Empty Deleted… or Delete All Records…). Deleting the app also removes its data.

Troubleshooting a request that never finishes

If a bubble stays on “Waiting for …”, check in this order:

  • The companion app shows as Installed and has a model loaded (not only downloaded).
  • Settings → Assistant provider is set to the app you installed, and “Shared storage” reads Ready.
  • Both apps were updated from the same developer account, then relaunch both.
  • After 120 seconds the bubble offers Check result and Retry; a retry gets a fresh request.
